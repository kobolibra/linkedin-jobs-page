ABOUT NEXT JOURNEY / WHAT WE DO

See the market.
Know where you stand.

Not a real-time alert for newly posted jobs.Not a service that matches roles to your résumé.Not a tool that rewrites your CV or cover letter for a job description.

Next Journey helps you see the bigger picture—and keep your own search in order.Explore where financial firms are hiring, filter and save roles worth considering, and record your applications, interviews and outcomes. My Journey turns that record into a Sankey diagram, so you can see how far you have come.

Listings come from LinkedIn, not employers' career sites, and are refreshed every six hours. This is neither live monitoring nor a complete record of available jobs; new openings may appear late or not at all.

08 / GOOD TO KNOW

A few practical details.

Why is a job description missing?＋

Full-description collection currently focuses on selected employers' roles in mainland China. Some Hong Kong and Singapore listings contain only summary details; visit the LinkedIn posting for more.

Is “first seen” the date a job was posted?＋

Not necessarily. It is the first date kept in this site's records. Some older entries use dates recovered from the source, and reposted roles may appear more recently.

Does “active” mean a job is still open?＋

Not always. “Active” means we have not marked it closed; “closed” does not prove it has been filled. Status changes may lag. Check the original posting before applying.

Can I apply directly through Next Journey?＋

No. Applications are not submitted or automated here. Open the LinkedIn listing and follow the employer's application instructions.
