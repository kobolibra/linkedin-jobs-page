03 / MY JOURNEY

Saved roles

Star jobs, use the saved-only filter, and exclude unwanted employers or title keywords. Opened job titles receive a read marker.

05 / DATA NOTES

Data notes

Does “first seen” mean the original posting date?＋

Not necessarily. It is the date retained as the first record on this site. Some historical entries use source dates, and reposts may appear among newer records.

Does “active” guarantee a role is open?＋

No. “Active” means the site has not marked it closed; “closed” does not mean filled. Always verify the original posting before applying.

Why is a job description missing?＋

Full-description collection currently focuses on selected mainland China roles. Some Hong Kong and Singapore listings contain only summary information.

Can I apply here?＋

No. Applications are neither submitted nor automated by Next Journey. Follow the link to LinkedIn and the employer's application instructions.
