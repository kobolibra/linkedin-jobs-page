05 / DATA NOTES

Data notes

Is “first seen” the original posting date?＋

Not necessarily. It is the earliest date retained in the site’s records; some historical entries use dates recovered from the source. Reposted roles may appear among newer listings.

Does “active” mean a role is still open?＋

Not necessarily. “Active” means the site has not marked the role as closed; “closed” does not mean the position has been filled. Check the original listing before applying.

Why are some job descriptions incomplete?＋

Full-description collection currently prioritizes selected roles in mainland China. Some Hong Kong and Singapore listings include only summary details; check LinkedIn for the original description.

Can I apply directly on Next Journey?＋

No. We don't submit applications. Open the original LinkedIn listing and follow the employer’s application instructions.
