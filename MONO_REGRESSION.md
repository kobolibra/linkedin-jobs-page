# Mono regression

The local live-data preview now uses the lieflat-charts Mono system: `#F0EFEB` paper background, `#1C1C1A` ink, `#8F8E88` muted text, `#C6C5BF` faint text, and `#DEDDD6` rules. The regional timeline uses a monochrome ladder for CN/HK/SG; the 30-day rhythm chart uses a charcoal panel with paper-gray line and nodes; Company Insight opens on a charcoal panel with grayscale metrics and distribution bars. Search, region tabs, time/company selectors, compact mode, favorites, hide-company controls, and Company Insight remain interactive on real data.
