## Deterministic safeguards

- A non-job posting neutralises every dimension.
- Insufficient evidence never becomes a confident profession guess.
- Primary Activity is a model judgement; archetype defaults are only a low-confidence fallback.
- Leadership is gated by explicit people/team evidence; title alone is not management evidence.
- Five independent structural risks are bounded and each emitted flag includes a reader-verifiable reason.
- The browser receives no provider secret; all provider calls use the same-origin Worker proxy.
